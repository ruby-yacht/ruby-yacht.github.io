Artists

An alphabetical selection of artists, researchers, and creative systems expanding relationships between computation and artistic practice.

AI Music Machinima Opera

Arido Taurajo

Arido Taurajo (2018–ongoing) is an operatic machinima created through human–AI collaboration. Roboccini, trained on Puccini’s music, generated its melody for a contemporary story staged inside World of Warcraft.

Image: Arido Taurajo, courtesy of the project collaborators.

Generative Art AARON

Harold Cohen

Cohen created AARON, a landmark artmaking program developed over decades to autonomously produce drawings and paintings from encoded artistic knowledge.

Image: AARON KCAT, via the Whitney Museum.

Memories of Passersby I (2018) is an autonomous machine that continuously generates uncanny portraits using neural networks.

Reas writes software as an artistic medium, allowing images, movement, and unexpected creative behavior to emerge from carefully constructed rules.

Machine Learning Datasets

Ridler treats datasets as creative material, often building hand-labeled archives whose structure, labor, and biases remain visible in the resulting work.

Image: Mosaic Virus 2019, courtesy of the artist.

Akten investigates machine interpretation and perception, using neural networks to reveal how learned systems construct meaning from limited data and experience.

Simon Colton

Colton developed The Painting Fool (2001–), a long-running creative system designed to make aesthetic decisions and be taken seriously as an artist.

Creative Robotics Embodied Learning

Rob Saunders

Saunders researches embodied computational creativity, including methods that translate dancers’ movement expertise into expressive behavior for non-humanlike robotic performers.

Image: Performative Body Mapping for Designing Expressive Robots.

Socially Engaged AI Storytelling

Stephanie Dinkins

Dinkins creates community-rooted AI projects that examine race, gender, power, and whose knowledge and lived experience become legible to technical systems.

Image: Conversations with BINA48, courtesy of the artist.

Artist Protection Adversarial ML

Glaze + Nightshade

Created by the University of Chicago SAND Lab, Glaze cloaks an artist’s style while Nightshade disrupts unauthorized model training by turning copied images into poisoned data.

Image: Glaze and Nightshade feature, University of Chicago News.

Critical Play Feminist AI

Mary Flanagan

Flanagan builds critical games and computational artworks, including [Grace:AI], a system trained on work by women artists that generates an evolving visual daydream.

Interactive Performance AI + Voice

Chloe Johnston / GRACE

GRACE revisits Johnston’s voice role in the AI-driven game Façade, exploring identity, authorship, natural-language systems, and what happens when a performer’s voice becomes technology.
